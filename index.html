Instant Receipt OCR

Snap a bill with the Action Button or camera. On-device Vision and Cloud AI extract line items, taxes, and amounts in seconds with privacy protection.

🔒

Biometric Travel Vault

Store encrypted passports, travel visas, boarding passes, and bookings behind Face ID or Touch ID. Includes smart 6-month expiry reminders and full offline access.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Designated Family Reps

Big multi-family trip? Not everyone wants to download an app or pay individually. One designated person manages and pays on behalf of their entire household, weighting shared costs by family headcount effortlessly.

🧳

Shared Packing & Itineraries

Coordinate day-by-day group activity schedules with time slots and notes. Build collaborative packing checklists with assigned travelers so nobody forgets passports, first aid, or power adapters.

📑

Calendar Sync & PDF/Excel Export

Export your day-by-day itinerary directly to Apple Calendar or import bookings via .ics. Export itemized expense reports as clean PDFs or download raw CSV data for Excel and accounting.

🗺️

Curated Itineraries & Smart Slots

Explore verified attractions, opening days, and operating hours across 11 global destinations (Tokyo, Singapore, Bali, Paris, and more) with proximity detection.

✈️

Offline Roaming Outbox

Log spending on airplanes, ferries, or in remote spots with zero cellular signal. Auto-syncs to your travel squad once reconnected.

🍏

Seamless Multi-Device Sync

Sign in with Apple to securely synchronize your trips, receipts, and vaults across your iPhone and iPad with end-to-end encryption and zero passwords.

💳

Couples & Joint Cards

Share a bank account or joint credit card without generating false internal debts between partners while fairly splitting the rest.

📊

Daily Burn Rate Gauge

Monitor your pace against your trip budget in real time with interactive charts and glanceable Home & Lock Screen widgets.

🤝

1-Tap Settlement Handoff

Settle debts instantly via Apple Cash in iMessage with pre-filled amounts, Australian PayID with auto-expiring clipboards, or PayPal & Revolut.
