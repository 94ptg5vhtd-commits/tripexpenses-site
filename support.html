Frequently Asked Questions

✈️ Does TripExpenses work offline while roaming?

Yes! TripExpenses is built with an offline-first architecture using SwiftData. You can add expenses, scan receipts, and calculate debt settlements on airplanes or in remote areas with zero cell signal. Once you reconnect to WiFi or cellular data, your changes automatically sync to your group in the background.

📷 How does the Camera Receipt Scanner work?

Tap the camera icon when logging an expense (or trigger the Action Button shortcut) to snap a paper bill or receipt. Apple's on-device Vision framework immediately analyzes the image, detects the total amount and currency, and extracts individual dish and drink line items so you never have to type manually.

💳 How does Joint Card splitting work for couples?

When you and your partner share an account or credit card, choose "Joint Card" as the payment method and select both partner names. TripExpenses credits your combined share without creating false internal debts between the two of you, while fairly splitting the remainder across your travel companions.

🤝 How do I settle debts with friends?

In the Balances tab, tap "Settle Up" on any friend to trigger native 1-tap handoffs: Apple Cash via iMessage with amount pre-filled, Australian PayID / Osko with 1-tap clipboard copying, or direct deep links to PayPal.me and Revolut.

🌐 Can Android friends view or join our trip?

Yes! Every shared trip includes a web link with a 6-character code. Android or desktop users can open the link in any modern web browser to view real-time balances, itemized expenses, and debt settlement options.

👑 How does the "Only Host Pays" Pro subscription work?

Only the trip creator needs a TripExpenses Pro subscription ($0.99/month or $9.99/year). All invited friends, family members, and travel companions can join, scan receipts, and log expenses 100% free without ever hitting a paywall.

🔄 How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the app, navigate to Settings > TripExpenses Pro, and tap "Restore Purchases". StoreKit 2 will automatically communicate with Apple's App Store and restore your active subscription.
